PalliQ · Account & Data Rights

Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your PalliQ account and associated personal information by contacting Pal Care Services using the steps on this page.

How to request account deletion

At present, account deletion requests are handled by email. An in-app account deletion option is not currently available.

  1. Open your email app. Use the email address linked to your PalliQ account, where possible.
  2. Send a deletion request. Use the subject line PalliQ account deletion request.
  3. Identify your account. Include the account email or mobile number and your account role (for example, patient/family caregiver or clinician). Do not send your password or OTP.
  4. Verification. We may contact you to verify that you own or are authorised to manage the account before processing the request.
Email an account deletion request ↗
Important: Sending a request does not instantly delete the account. We will verify the request and process it through our administrator/backend workflow.

What happens to your information?

We will review the request and delete the account and associated personal information where applicable. Certain information may need to be retained when required by law or for legitimate medical-record obligations. Daily backups may temporarily retain information until the relevant backup cycle expires.

Request deletion of specific data

To request deletion of some personal information without deleting your entire account, email us using the same address and explain which information you want us to review.
