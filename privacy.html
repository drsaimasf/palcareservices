01Who we are

PalliQ is an AI-powered continuous care platform for people living with serious illness, their caregivers, and the clinical teams who support them. PalliQ is developed by Pal Care Services Private Limited, a company incorporated in 2025 and based in Delhi, India. Pal Care Services is responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

PalliQ is currently in active development. Some features described on our website may not yet be available, and this policy will be updated as features are released.

02Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to provide PalliQ and to respond to you. Depending on how you use PalliQ, this may include the categories below.

Personal information

  • Information provided during sign-up, including your name, email address, and mobile number, depending on the registration method.
  • Profile information that may be added or updated after sign-up, including age, date of birth, gender, address, and emergency contact details.

Account details

  • Account identifiers and authentication information. PalliQ supports email OTP, mobile OTP, and email address with password. Firebase is used for authentication.
  • Your role in the app (for example, patient, caregiver, doctor, or nurse) and the care team you are linked to.
  • Settings and preferences, such as reminder and notification settings.

Patient and health information

PalliQ is designed to support care between visits. To do this, it may process sensitive health information that you, your caregiver, or your care team enter, such as:

  • Symptom check-ins and symptom journal entries.
  • Medications and medication reminders.
  • Care plans, care goals, interventions, functional status, and outcomes over time.
  • Clinical notes and AI-generated summaries based on daily check-ins.
  • Pain scores, care-plan information, care goals, appointment or call details, and patient-doctor chat information, where these features are used.

We treat health information as sensitive and process it only to provide care-related features and as described in this policy.

Appointment and communication data

  • Appointment and call details, where recorded or scheduled through PalliQ.
  • Messages and communications between patients, caregivers, and care teams through in-app chat, where used.
  • Messages you send us for support, feedback, or enquiries.

Device and technical information

  • Device type, operating system and version, app version, and language settings, where collected by the app or its providers.
  • Push notification tokens used to deliver reminders and alerts through Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Diagnostic, crash, and usage information only where the relevant collection tools are enabled.

PalliQ may request notification permission, camera or microphone access for calls or video calls, and photo or file access for uploads, when you use the related feature. You can manage applicable permissions in your device settings.

Information from this website

  • Query form. If you use the query form on our website, we receive your name, email address, optional organisation, and your message. Form submissions are processed by our form provider, Formspree, and delivered to our inbox.
  • Fonts. Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address and browser details with Google.
  • Hosting. Our website is hosted by Netlify, which may process standard technical information such as IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the website.

Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

03How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Create and manage your account and verify your identity.
  • Provide PalliQ features, such as check-ins, medication reminders, symptom journaling, care plans, and trend summaries for your care team.
  • Help authorised care teams understand changes in your condition and prioritise care.
  • Send reminders, alerts, and service-related notifications.
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide support.
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve PalliQ.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

AI-supported summaries. PalliQ uses AI features to generate summaries from daily check-ins. The AI system has been trained for this purpose. Health information is processed to generate these summaries and support care-related features. The company has stated that it does not use data for research. This does not imply that identifiable patient records are used to train the model unless that practice is separately confirmed.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use health information for advertising.

04How we share information

We share personal information only as needed to provide PalliQ, and only with:

  • Your care team and caregivers — doctors, nurses, and caregivers you are linked with in the app, so they can support your care.
  • Healthcare organisations — hospitals, NGOs, or institutions providing your care through PalliQ, where applicable.
  • Service providers — MySQL database services, PythonAnywhere backend hosting, Firebase Authentication, Twilio for SMS OTP, Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications, and Resend for email, as applicable to the feature being used.
  • Legal and safety reasons — where required by law, court order, or government authority, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of patients, users, or others.
  • Business changes — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or transfer of assets, subject to this policy.

The company has confirmed India as the data storage region. Some third-party service providers may process limited information using their own infrastructure and terms; actual processing locations depend on the services used.

05Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration. Access to health information is limited to people who need it to provide care or operate the service.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If we become aware of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

06Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy, including providing care-related services, meeting legal, medical-record, or regulatory requirements, and resolving disputes. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

Database backups are performed daily. Account deletion is handled by the administrator or backend process after a deletion request. The company has stated that health records are retained until a deletion request. Information may still need to be retained where applicable law requires it. Daily backups may temporarily contain information until the relevant backup cycle expires.

07Your choices & deletion requests

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete your account and personal information.
  • Withdraw consent you have given, where processing is based on consent.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity, where applicable.

To request account or data deletion, email director@palcareservicespvtltd.co.in with the subject “PalliQ data deletion request” from the email address linked to your account. We may need to verify your identity before acting on the request. Account deletion is handled through the administrator/backend process; contact us by email to request deletion.

Some information may need to be retained after a deletion request where required by law or for legitimate medical-record obligations. We will tell you if this applies.

You can also turn off push notifications in your device settings at any time.

08Children's privacy

PalliQ supports paediatric patients. An account may be created by a parent/legal guardian or a family caregiver, but the guardian completes the child's consent declaration. The consent declaration is stored in the backend. To withdraw consent, contact Pal Care Services using the contact details below. The parent or lawful guardian should manage the child's account and consent on the child's behalf.

09Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, especially as PalliQ features are released. We will post the updated version on this page with a new “Last updated” date and, where changes are significant, notify you in the app or by email.
